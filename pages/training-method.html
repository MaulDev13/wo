Training Methods Map 🧠

Framework murni kategori metode latihan fisik. Semua program fitness hanyalah kombinasi dari metode-metode ini.

Goal: Understand fundamental training methods

1. Resistance Training 🏋️

Latihan melawan beban untuk meningkatkan strength, hypertrophy, dan muscular endurance.

Type Contoh Fokus
Dynamic (concentric/eccentric) Squat, deadlift, bench press, push-up Strength & hypertrophy
Isometric Plank, wall sit, hollow hold, dead hang Stability & endurance
Bodyweight resistance Pull-up, dip, calisthenics basic Relative strength
External load Barbell, dumbbell, machine Progressive overload

2. Cardiovascular Training 🫀

Melatih sistem jantung dan paru untuk endurance dan recovery capacity.

Type Contoh Fokus
Steady state Jogging, cycling santai Aerobic base
Interval cardio Run-walk, tempo run VO₂ max
Long endurance Long distance running Durability

3. Metabolic Conditioning (MetCon / HIIT) ⚡

Metode intensitas tinggi yang menggabungkan kerja otot + sistem kardio.

Format Contoh Fokus
HIIT 30 detik kerja / 15 detik istirahat Fat loss & conditioning
EMOM Every minute on the minute Work capacity
AMRAP As many reps as possible Muscular endurance
Circuit training Multi-exercise loop Full body conditioning

4. Movement Skill Training 🤸

Latihan kontrol tubuh, koordinasi, dan efisiensi gerakan.

Type Contoh Fokus
Static skill Handstand, L-sit Balance & control
Dynamic skill Muscle-up, explosive pull-up Power + coordination
Body control Ring stability, calisthenics flow Neuromuscular efficiency

5. Functional Movement Training 🧱

Latihan berbasis pola gerakan manusia sehari-hari.

Pattern Contoh Fokus
Push / Pull Push-up, row Upper body strength
Squat Squat, lunge Lower body strength
Hinge Deadlift pattern Posterior chain
Carry Farmer carry Grip & core stability
Locomotion Bear crawl, burpee Total body endurance

6. Mobility & Recovery 🧘

Metode untuk meningkatkan range of motion dan mempercepat recovery.

Type Contoh Fokus
Static stretching Hamstring stretch Flexibility
Dynamic mobility Hip opener, shoulder circles Joint control
Recovery work Breathing drills, yoga Recovery & injury prevention

Summary 🧠

Semua latihan fisik di dunia ini hanya kombinasi dari 6 metode dasar:

🏋️ Resistance
🫀 Cardio
⚡ Metabolic Conditioning (HIIT)
🤸 Movement Skill
🧱 Functional Movement
🧘 Mobility & Recovery

Tidak ada sistem latihan baru di luar ini — hanya cara berbeda menggabungkannya.
